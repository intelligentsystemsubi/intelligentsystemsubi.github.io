Cristiano Patrício is a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Beira Interior and NOVA LINCS, supported by a national research grant from FCT. His research focuses on developing inherently interpretable deep learning models for disease diagnosis in medical imaging, with interests in Explainable AI, Vision-Language Models and Medical Image Analysis. Cristiano has authored 10 research papers in international conferences and journals, which have garnered more than 200 citations.

Cristiano Patrício received a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (17/20) in 2019 from the Polytechnic of Guarda and his M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (18/20) in 2021 from the University of Beira Interior. He received 1 Merit Scholarship in the 2018/2019 academic year and was one of the winners of the Huawei Scholarship Program in 2023. During his third year of Ph.D., Cristiano spent four months as a visiting Ph.D. student at the Computer Science Department of the University of Turin, hosted by the EIDOSLab research group. Cristiano is currently a Research Assistant at INESC TEC and an Invited Assistant at the University of Beira Interior. Previously, Cristiano participated in developing solutions for the Altice Portugal Foundation (MagicContact Web) projects and for the “Perception for a Service Robot” project of NOVA LINCS.
